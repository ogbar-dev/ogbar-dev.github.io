ControlDeck

Sliders and buttons for volume, brightness and media on your PCs and tablets, over your local network.

Android

Get ControlDeck from Google Play on your phone or tablet.

Windows agent

To control a Windows PC, run the free ControlDeck agent on it:

  1. Download ControlDeck-Windows-v<version>.zip from the latest release.
  2. Unzip it anywhere and start ControlDeck.Agent.exe. No installer and no account.
  3. In the Android app open Devices, select the PC and pair it with the QR code or PIN the PC shows.

Both devices have to be on the same Wi-Fi / local network.
