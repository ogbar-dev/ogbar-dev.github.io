Picture Dictionary (Képes szótár) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Picture Dictionary (Képes szótár) ("the app") is a Hungarian and English picture dictionary for small children, made by Ogbar ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app from Google Play.

The short version

The app collects no personal data, from children or from adults. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no server of its own. Every photo and every voice recording is built into the app, so it works without an internet connection.

What the app keeps on your device

  • The parent code. The 4-digit code that unlocks the child lock is stored on the device only as a salted cryptographic hash, never as the code itself. It is excluded from Android backup and never leaves the device.
  • The chosen language (Hungarian or English).
  • Whether the topics have been unlocked, so a purchase keeps working offline.

None of this is sent anywhere. I never receive it.

Purchases (Google Play)

The animals are free. The other topics can be unlocked with a one-time purchase through Google Play. The purchase can only be started from the parent area, behind the parent code. Google Play handles the payment; I never see your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that your Google account owns the unlock, so it can open the topics, also after a reinstall.

Children

The app is made for children. It is designed so that a child cannot leave it, see an ad, make a purchase, open a link or reach any other app without the parent code. It does not collect, store or share any personal information about children.

Photos and voices

The photographs come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences; their authors are credited inside the app (parent area → Photo credits). The voice recordings are synthetic and built into the app.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. A purchase stays with your Google account and is restored if you install the app again.
