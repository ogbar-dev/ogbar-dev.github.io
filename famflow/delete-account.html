FamFlow — Delete your account

FamFlow: Family Organizer, by Ogbar

In the app

  1. Open FamFlow and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password, or with your Google account if you signed in with Google.

If you have not joined a family yet, the same Delete account button is on the first screen.

By e-mail

If you can no longer open the app, write to ogbar.dev@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your FamFlow account, with the subject "FamFlow account deletion". Your account is deleted within 30 days and you get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your sign-in account (e-mail address, name), your membership in the family and your personal settings.
  • Kept: the family's shared history of what was done together — for example that a task was completed or a reward was redeemed — so the other members' points still add up. It no longer belongs to an account.
  • When the last member of a family deletes their account, all of the family's data is deleted.
  • A FamFlow Plus subscription is not cancelled by deleting the account: cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
